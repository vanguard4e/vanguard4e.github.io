We're keeping recruitment open. It's only week one, and we've already had a fair bit of movement in the roster. If you're interested, apply! We're planning to accept trials for a handful more people than the raid needs, but we're not recruiting raid spots endlessly. The goal is a little breathing room for people dropping off or plans changing, without overfilling the guild, so we're ready to raid on night one.

We don't bench or cut people without a good reason. As content goes on farm, though, we expect performance to keep up. Know your class, show up prepared, and improve as the fights get easier.
