Who We Are

Welcome to High Guard

High Guard is an Alliance · US PvE raiding guild on WoW Forever. Most of us are working adults with limited time to play. We don't raid six nights a week. We raid two nights, show up prepared, and make those nights count.

The name wasn't an accident.

A high guard is the shield raised first and held the longest — the people the rest of the raid can count on.

Every one of us is a longtime WoW player. Some of us have been here since the original Vanilla launch back in 2004, and the rest joined somewhere in between. Our founding core of 10 didn't all raid together before this. We're friends of friends who came together for this one-of-a-kind launch, from Mythic raiders in retail to serious PvPers, so the experience in this group runs deep and wide.

Being out front doesn't mean living in the game. We raid on a light schedule, with no raging raid leads, and we don't carry people who'd be happier on alt runs. And no loot drama. EPGP keeps every drop transparent and fair, so nobody's arguing over who deserves what.

We take the time we have seriously, because how each of us shows up affects everyone around us.

Show up ready

Gems, enchants, consumables, and a quick look at the fight before we pull.

Play hard

Learn a bit each week. Take a note, fix the mistake, and pull again.

Have fun

Wipes happen. No blame spirals. Good people who play to win.
