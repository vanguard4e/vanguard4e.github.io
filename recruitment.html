Recruitment

Trials & Ranks

  1. Apply. Read through this site, then hop into Discord and click Apply in 📝︱recruitment. A private channel opens with you and our officers. Answer a few quick questions and you're in as a Member.
  2. Earn a seat as a Trial Raider. Trials run 3–6 weeks.
  3. Loot from night one. Trials are loot-eligible from their very first raid. No waiting period.
  4. Officer vote. Sometime during your trial, the officers and the GM vote on your promotion to Raider. A new member needs 4 of the 5 officer votes. We look at attitude, effort, and performance, and we hope every trial sticks.
