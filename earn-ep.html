Guild bank deposits

Stock the bank with things the raid uses and earn EP. The Bank Officer verifies every deposit before EP is awarded. Values are based on market value and kept deliberately modest.

DepositEP
Herbs, raw mats (ore, leather, cloth), combat potions, buff food0.5 per 2
Combat elixirs0.5 each
Black Lotus2 each
Raid feasts5 each
Flasks7.5 each
Gold1 per 10g

Weekly cap: 77.5 EP from deposits. Healing potions don't count (they're First Aid in Forever), and healthstones are a warlock service, not a deposit.
