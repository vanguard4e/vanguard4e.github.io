The bench is a roster call, not a punishment

  • If you show up and get benched, you get full EP, boss kills included. No difference from raiding.
  • Benched nights count as attendance toward the 85%.
  • Nobody gets benched two weeks in a row. We ask for volunteers at the start of the night.
  • If you're the only one with a class or spec we need, the Raid Lead may pull you in.
  • Can't make it? Post in #absences before the raid. No-shows who don't post get 0 EP.

What we ask on raid night

  • Be in voice 10–15 minutes before invites
  • Bring gems, enchants, and consumables
  • Skim the fight before we pull
  • Know your class and your role
  • Keep an eye on Discord for updates
  • Pugs follow the same consumable and loot rules
